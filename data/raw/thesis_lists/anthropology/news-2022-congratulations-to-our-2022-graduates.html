Congratulations to Our 2022 Graduates!

June 15, 2022

Undergraduate Anthropology awards and honors were announced at the department graduation celebration June, 13, 2022. There are as follows:

 

Phi Beta Kappa Honors Society Electees:

Emily Apadula

Madelyn Moy

Chloe Ponzio

Taylor Shineman

Bengi Rwabuhemba

 

Thesis Honors Recipients:

 

Chloe Ponzio

Bringing home spot: domestication and human-dog relationships across the ancient Americas

Advisor: Cynthia Robin

Award: Elizabeth M. Brumfiel Award for Distinguished Honors Thesis in Archaeology

 

Ellie Thomson

Armed with syringes: martial metaphors and the militarization of COVID-19 interventions

Advisor: Peter Locke

Award: Friends of Anthropology Award for Distinguished Honors Thesis in Public Health​

 

Madelyn Moy

Long term effects of hepatitis-B infection on the gut microbiome of captive chimpanzees (Pan troglodytes)

Advisor: Katie Amato

Award: Oswald Werner Prize for Distinguished Honors Thesis in Anthropology
