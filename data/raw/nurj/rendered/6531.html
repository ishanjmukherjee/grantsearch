LETTER FROM THE EDITOR

In this edition of the NURJ Spring 2023-2024, we are excited to share the works of both undergraduates and our very own NURJ team. From artwork to research in the humanities and scientific fields, this volume is truly diverse in form and content. Research is, in nature, an evolving field, and the NURJ is ever dedicated to presenting it as such. To our writers and readers alike, thank you for joining us on our journey to explore the diverse topics that contribute to the forefront of technological innovation and understanding of the world around us. As always, thank you for your support.
