FROM THE EDITORS

This fall, the Northwestern Interdisciplinary Law Review (NILR) enters its sixth year of publication. The Review's first five volumes have established the journal as a consistent and accessible source of interdisciplinary legal scholarship, drawing from fields such as history, literature, economics, art theory, and philosophy. Working alongside talented law students, practitioners, and professors, consecutive classes of Northwestern University students have carved out a space for substantial legal dialogue amongst scholars of various disciplines and perspectives. The current editorial board feels privileged to carry on this tradition and remains committed to featuring the same high-quality literature that readers have come to expect from the NILR.
