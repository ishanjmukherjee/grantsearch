The Northwestern Interdisciplinary Law Review (ISSN 1945-2233) is currently published once annually in the spring. Every volume is usually comprised of 7-8 articles, for a total length of approximately 300 pages.

Annual print subscriptions: $15 per year (including shipping & handling costs).

      A partial list of law libraries recently adding the NILR include:

  • Berkeley Law
  • University of California Hastings Law
  • Univ. of Chicago Law
  • Duke Law
  • Harvard Law
  • Univ. of Michigan Law
  • Univ. of Minnesota Law
  • NYU Law
  • Univ. of Pennsylvania Law
  • Univ. of Virginia Law
  • USC Law

Published each spring, the NILR is a legal periodical focused on publishing excellent interdisciplinary scholarship dealing with issues at the nexus of law and the humanities, law and the sciences, and law and society.
