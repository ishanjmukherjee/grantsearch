Faculty Newsletter | December 2025

Dear faculty,

 

As we approach winter recess, I think we can all agree that 2025 has been another year we won't soon forget. Yet, despite challenges, we have kept our collective eye on ensuring that Northwestern remains a world-class research institution that makes our society better, while educating our talented students and supporting our exceptional faculty and staff. I believe it is this shared vision that carried us through some of the toughest moments this University has seen, and will keep us going for generations to come.

 

I want to emphasize that the past year saw us launch a range of important initiatives, institutes and centers; produce breakthrough research; win prestigious awards (including a Nobel Prize); and continue to provide a welcoming and intellectually rigorous environment for our students. As always, this high level of achievement has been led by you, our faculty. You have demonstrated time and time again your commitment to your work, your students and the University, and I could not be prouder of all we have accomplished together, even under unusually challenging circumstances.

 

Thank you for your dedication, and I wish you all a restful winter recess and a happy new year!

 

Sincerely,

 

Kathleen Hagerty
Provost 
First Chicago Professor of Finance

Opportunities through the Roberta Buffett Institute

The Roberta Buffett Institute offers a range of programs for faculty. The following have deadlines coming soon:

  • Become a Buffett Faculty Fellow. These fellowships support faculty who need to be away from the University to conduct international research. Deadline Jan. 12. 
  • Sponsor a Buffett Visiting Scholar. Receive logistical support as you help a visiting scholar gain access to Northwestern's multidisciplinary, global community. Deadline Jan. 15.
  • Work with the Buffett Undergraduate Research Fellowship Program. This program awards grants for faculty to hire undergraduate students to support their global or international research. Deadline Jan. 15.
  • Start a Buffett Global Working Group. Support Northwestern faculty working on a global problem in an interdisciplinary context. Grant offers up to $200K spread over two to five years. Contact Harrington Weihl, Buffett's Director for Research, to discuss proposals. Deadline Feb. 16.

 

Registration open for NUIT's winter Research Computing and Data Services workshops

Acquire new coding skills, upgrade your existing skill set and learn how to maximize your research efforts at one of Northwestern IT Research Computing and Data Services' winter sessions. Choose from a variety of workshops, either virtual or in person (Chicago), including AI for Research, Next Steps in Python, Statistics, and Data Management. Learn more and register and subscribe to the NUIT-RESEARCH listserv to receive announcements of upcoming workshops and events. 

 

 

2025 tax year reminders

To help ensure you receive a correct 2025 W-2 form, be sure to review pay stubs and/or myHR for basic information accuracy. This includes:

  • Spelling of your legal name
  • Correct SSN
  • Correct home address (the deadline to update your address in myHR  is Dec. 19, 2025)

You can review and change your W-2 delivery option in myHR. Changes to your W-2 delivery option must be made no later than Dec. 31, 2025. More information can be found on the central HR website.
