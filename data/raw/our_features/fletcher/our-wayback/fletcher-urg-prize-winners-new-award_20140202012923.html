Fletcher URG Prize Winners (New Award!)

Huge congratulations are in order for the inaugural winners of the Fletcher URG Prize.  The award seeks to honors the best Summer URG projects of the year as nominated by the faculty sponsors and judged by the URG Committee.  Give a big slap on the back of happiness to Daniel Olson, Chelsea Glenn, Emily DuBois, and Ewain Gwynn when you see them next.  In addition to a cash prize, they will attend a luncheon in their honor with Morty Shapiro and Dan Linzer along with their fabulous sponsors.  Kind of makes you want to get involved, doesn't it?
