To Members of the Northwestern Community:


Today marks the fourth annual Undergraduate Research Symposium at Northwestern University. The exceptional scholarly and creative accomplishments of more than 100 of our undergraduates are on display in the form of posters and presentations. Northwestern is extraordinarily proud of these students and the work that they have done. Projects from across the University are represented: natural sciences, humanities, engineering, social sciences, and fine arts.


The time, energy, and thought that went into the creation of this work are remarkable. Equally praiseworthy is the investment made by the faculty mentors of these students. Northwestern faculty members have again shown themselves to be eager to help students become active participants in the scholarly and creative work for which they, our faculty, are justly celebrated.


I know you look forward as I do to a day of exciting and captivating new ideas.


Sincerely yours,


Lawrence B. Dumas

Provost
