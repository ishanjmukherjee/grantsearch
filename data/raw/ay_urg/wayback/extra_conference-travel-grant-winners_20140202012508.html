Congratulations Conference Travel Grant Winners!

Xuchen Han, WCAS, Mathematics, Economics, Mathematical Methods in the Social Sciences

“Novel Approach for Evaluating Phases and Orientations of Polycrystalline Structures” project to be presented at the Joint Mathematics Meetings conference hosted by the American Mathematical Society and the Mathematical Association of America. Faculty Sponsor: Michael Stein

Iseli Hernandez, WCAS, Psychology

“Culture’s Influence on Parental Health Beliefs: An Exploration of Behavioral Endorsements” poster to be presented at the Cognitive Development Society Meeting in Memphis, TN. Faculty Sponsor: Karl Rosengren

Kelsey Jorgensen, McCormick, Materials Science and Engineering

“The Binary Join of the Transparent Conducting Oxides GITO and ZITO” project to be presented at the MS&T conference in Montreal, Canada. Faculty Sponsor: Thomas Mason

Viktorya Tannenbaum, WCAS, Biochemistry, International Studies

“Measuring Surgical Capacity of Faith-Based Health Facilities in India” project to be presented at the World Health Organization Emergency and Essential Surgical Care meeting in Trinidad and Tobago. Faculty Sponsor: Meena Cherian

John Yang, WCAS

“Evaluation of Novel Nanobins Loaded with Arsenic and Cisplatin for Targeted Drug Delivery” poster to be presented at the 2013 Sigma Xi Student Research Society Conference in North Carolina. Faculty Sponsor: Thomas O’Halloran

Jessica Yu, McCormick, Chemical Engineering

“Agent-Based Models of Heterogeneous Tumor Cell Populations” paper to be presented at the 2013 AIChE Annual Meeting in San Francisco, CA. Faculty Sponsor: Neda Bagheri
