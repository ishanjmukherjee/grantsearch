Congratulations to January URG Winners!

2010-11 Academic Year Undergraduate Research Grant Winners, 4th cycle

Maria Dangles (Art History, Psychology), “Demystifying Jackson Pollock: Enhancing Novice Appreciation of Abstract Art with Wall Text” Advisor: David Rapp, Psychology

Sarah Hong (Political Science), “The Progression of Sino-ROK Relations and its Effects on the Prospects for Reunification on the Korean Peninsula” Advisor: Jonathan Caverley, Political Science

Nadine Ibrahim (Communications Sciences & Disorders), “Absolute and relative pitch in singers: Comparisons of voice F0 responses to pitch-shift onset and offset conditions” Advisor: Chuck Larson, Communications Sciences & Disorders

Yuri Malina (Integrated Science Program), “Testing the Effects of Ultrasound Exposure on Plant Development and Hormone Production in Arabidopsis Thaliana” Advisor: Nyree Zerega, Biological Sciences

Christina Panton (Psychology, Global Health), “Improving Therapy Outcomes by Enhancing Learning During Sleep” Advisor: Richard Zinbarg, Psychology
