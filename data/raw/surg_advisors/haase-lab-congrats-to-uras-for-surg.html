04/18/202404/18/2024 | yln0241

Lily Hoyt: Nostalgic Notes: Music & Marriage

Angela Zhong: Exploring Physiological Linkages and Empathic Accuracy in Parent-Adolescent Conflict Conversations: A Study on Emotion Regulation and Relationship Dynamics

Lillian Fu: Interpersonal Emotional Acceptance and Physiological Linkage

Congrats to them!
